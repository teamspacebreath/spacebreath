About SpaceBreath

Four friends.
One shared practice.

We’ve been practising breathwork for more than three years. It has brought us joy, connection and a deeper sense of aliveness, and showed us how much thoughtful guidance can change the experience.

SpaceBreath grew from that discovery. Jonas develops and guides our English sessions, while Sylvia guides our Spanish sessions and directs and records our videos. Harry composes the original music, and Jonas and Harry edit the videos together. Erich creates our thumbnails, animations, logo and wider visual identity.

Our hope is simple: to make meaningful breathwork experiences that help you pause, feel and reconnect.

Jonas, Harry, Sylvia & Erich

Practise with care

Your breath.
Your pace.

Always listen to your body and return to normal breathing if you feel uncomfortable. Practise seated or lying down in a safe place, never while driving, in water or anywhere a loss of awareness could put you at risk.
