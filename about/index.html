Four friends.

We make guided breathwork as a complete experience: the practice, the voice, the original score and the visual world around it.

The SpaceBreath story

Made from curiosity,
friendship and breath.

We’ve been practising breathwork for more than three years. It has brought us joy, connection and a deeper sense of aliveness, and showed us how much thoughtful guidance can change the experience.

SpaceBreath grew from that discovery. What began with Jonas and Harry’s long-running friendship and shared love of music became a wider creative practice with Sylvia and Erich.

Our hope is simple: to make meaningful breathwork experiences that help you pause, feel and reconnect.
