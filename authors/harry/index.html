About the work

Music that breathes with you

Every SpaceBreath score is made for a specific session rather than added afterwards. Harry works from the breathing structure, using tempo, time signatures, texture and dynamics to support the active rounds, transitions and holds.

His writing blends ambient electronic production, acoustic colours and field textures, with the aim of helping the guidance, breath and music feel like one connected journey.
