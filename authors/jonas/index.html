About the work

From intention to practice

Each session begins with an intention and a breathing structure. Jonas writes and practises the journey, refining its pace, cues and holds before recording the final guidance.

That framework then becomes the foundation for the original music, film and visual design, helping every part of a SpaceBreath session move together.
