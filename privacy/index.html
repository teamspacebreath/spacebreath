What we collect when you write to us or join our email updates, and how we look after it.

Last updated: 12 October 2026

Who we are

SpaceBreath is an independent, UK-based creative project making guided breathwork sessions with original music. We are responsible for the personal data described on this page.

For any privacy question or request, email us at hello@space-breath.com.

What we collect

Contact form. When you send us a message we receive your name, email address and message. We use them only to read and reply to you.

Email updates. If you sign up for updates we store your email address, the language you signed up in and when you subscribed. We use this to send news about new sessions, usually every other Tuesday. Every email includes an unsubscribe link.

We do not use analytics or advertising trackers, this website does not set its own cookies, and we never sell or share your details for marketing.

Services we use

Kit (Kit / ConvertKit LLC, United States) stores our email subscriber list and sends our email updates. See Kit’s privacy policy.

Formspree (Formspree, Inc., United States) receives contact form messages and forwards them to us. Formspree hosts data with Amazon Web Services in the United States.

Where these providers process data outside the UK, they rely on recognised safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses.

YouTube. Our session videos are hosted on YouTube. Nothing loads from YouTube until you press play on a video. When you do, the player is loaded from YouTube’s privacy-enhanced domain (youtube-nocookie.com), and Google may store data on your device and process information about your viewing under its own privacy policy.

Hosting. This website is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.) and delivered through Cloudflare. Like any web host, they process your IP address and basic request details to deliver pages and protect the site from abuse. Cloudflare may set a strictly necessary security cookie for this purpose.

Links to YouTube, Instagram and Patreon take you to those platforms, where their own policies apply.

Legal basis and retention

We process contact messages to respond to your request (legitimate interests) and send email updates on the basis of your consent, which you can withdraw at any time by unsubscribing or emailing us. We keep messages for as long as needed to handle your enquiry and keep your email address for updates until you unsubscribe.

Your rights

Under UK and EU data protection law you have the right to access, correct or delete your data, to restrict or object to its processing, to data portability, and to withdraw consent. To use any of these rights, email hello@space-breath.com. You can also complain to the UK Information Commissioner’s Office (ico.org.uk) or the data protection authority where you live.
