Session overview

At a glance

This 13-minute morning routine contains three different rounds. Round one uses steady natural breathing and a one-minute exhale hold. Round two increases the pace and ends with a 30-second inhale hold. Round three slows down and closes with a 90-second exhale hold.

Choosing your practice

Who this session is for

A balanced morning practice for people who want both grounding and activation rather than an entirely fast or entirely slow session.

Guided session

Breathe with us

Sit or lie down somewhere safe and comfortable. Keep the breath within a manageable range and return to natural breathing whenever needed.

Before you begin

Safety and contraindications

This session is for education and general wellbeing and is not medical advice. Do not practise while driving, in or near water, or anywhere a loss of consciousness could be dangerous. Do not practise if you are pregnant or have epilepsy, a cardiovascular condition or a serious respiratory condition. Consult a qualified healthcare professional if you are unsure whether breathwork is suitable for you.

If you feel uncomfortable, dizzy or unwell, stop the practice, return to natural breathing and rest.

The intention

A complete arc for the beginning of the day

The practice is designed to create balance by combining a grounded opening, a brighter middle and a slower finish.

The first round establishes awareness, the second introduces more active breathing and the final round gives the body time to settle again.

Gratitude is offered as a reflective intention rather than a required feeling. Notice what is present, keep the breath comfortable and let the practice meet you as you are.

Welcome and technique

Good morning, and welcome to another session with SpaceBreath. Today we are moving through a short and simple morning routine with three rounds, each using its own pace and a gentle hold at the end.

Sit upright with a relaxed, straight spine so the tummy and chest can expand freely. We breathe in through the nose and out through the mouth, although you can switch if another route feels better.

Round one

Begin with deep breaths, filling the tummy before lifting gently into the chest and releasing softly. Imagine each inhale bringing fresh energy and every exhale releasing tension.

Connect the inhale and exhale naturally without a gap. Take a big inhale, fully exhale and stay after the exhale for one minute. Rest in stillness and soften the shoulders, neck and face.

Round two

The second round is the energiser. Increase the pace while continuing to focus on the inhale: tummy, chest, then a soft release without a full exhale.

Take your deepest comfortable inhale and hold. Engage from the pelvic floor upwards through the body. Three, two, one. Fully exhale and let go.

Round three and closing

For the final cool-down, take deep, slow breaths. Notice the stretch in the tummy and chest, then release all tension as you breathe out.

Take the deepest comfortable inhale of the session and let go completely. Stay after the exhale for one minute 30 seconds, observing any tension or thoughts without needing to change them.

Inhale, hold briefly and release. Return to natural nasal breathing, begin small movements and take a morning stretch. Thank you for being part of the journey. See you soon.

Quick answers

Before you begin

Practical answers about the pattern, structure and how to approach the session.

How are the three rounds different?

Round one grounds with steady breathing, round two increases the pace and round three returns to deep, slow breaths.

How long are the holds?

The rounds end with a one-minute exhale hold, a 30-second inhale hold and a 90-second exhale hold.

Is this an intense morning session?

It includes one active round, but the opening and closing are slower. Reduce the pace or return to natural breathing whenever needed.
