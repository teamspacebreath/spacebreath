Before you begin

Safety and contraindications

This session is for education and general wellbeing and is not medical advice. Do not practise while driving, in or near water, or anywhere a loss of consciousness could be dangerous. Do not practise if you are pregnant or have epilepsy, a cardiovascular condition or a serious respiratory condition. Consult a qualified healthcare professional if you are unsure whether breathwork is suitable for you.

If you feel uncomfortable, dizzy or unwell, stop the practice, return to natural breathing and rest.

The intention

A deliberate way to begin the morning

This is not a claim that breathwork replaces coffee. It is a short ritual for checking in with your energy before the day gathers momentum.

The practice starts gently so you can arrive in your body, then increases the pace in stages. A soft, sighing exhale keeps the active rhythm from becoming unnecessarily tense.

Use it on its own or alongside the morning routine that already works for you. The purpose is presence and activation, not forcing a particular feeling.

Welcome and technique

Good morning, and welcome to another session with SpaceBreath. Today we are moving through a short morning breathwork routine. It is one continuous round that slowly increases in pace, with a short hold on the inhale and a longer hold on the exhale at the end.

The breathing pattern uses a soft sigh on the exhale. Bring the lips together as if you are about to pronounce a quiet P. I like to begin by breathing in through the nose and out through the mouth, but you can use the route that feels best for you.

Please sit upright or lie down. We begin in three, two, one.

Slow and medium stages

Be present with the practice. Feel the air entering the tummy and chest, and notice how the lips touch on every exhale. Arrive in this moment.

Take a strong, deep inhale. Hold briefly, then fully let go. Now we pick up the pace. Follow the pattern, stay conscious and keep the inhales full and the exhales releasing.

Fast stage and holds

Take a strong inhale and hold. Gently engage the pelvic floor and abdominal area. Fully exhale, then move into the faster rhythm. Keep the inhales strong and the exhales short.

Take a final deep inhale and fully exhale. We stay here after the exhale for one minute. Be the observer. Notice what is happening inside you and in the space around you.

Closing

Three, two, one. Deep inhale and hold for a moment, then fully let go.

Notice the space around you again and return to natural breathing through the nose. Thank you for being part of today’s practice. I hope you feel energised and refreshed. See you soon.

Quick answers

Before you begin

Practical answers about the pattern, structure and how to approach the session.

How long is the active morning practice?

The complete video lasts eight minutes. The continuous breathing round itself lasts approximately five minutes.

What is the breathing pattern?

The round begins slowly, increases through two faster stages, then finishes with a 30-second inhale hold and a one-minute exhale hold.

Does this replace coffee?

No. The title presents breathwork as another possible morning ritual, not as a medical or nutritional replacement for caffeine.
