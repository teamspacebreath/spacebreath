Session overview

At a glance

This 30-minute session moves through four rounds of fast, steady connected breathing. Each round ends with a 30 to 45-second inhale hold followed by an exhale hold that builds from one minute 30 seconds to two minutes 45 seconds.

Choosing your practice

Who this session is for

An extended, higher-intensity practice for experienced breathwork participants who are comfortable with fast mouth breathing and long breath holds.

Time

Thirty minutes

Leave enough time to settle before the session and rest afterwards.

Intention

Deep self-connection

A psychedelic-inspired journey designed for inward attention and extended stillness.

Experience

Advanced intensity

Choose a shorter or gentler practice if fast breathing or long holds are new to you.

Guided session

Breathe with us

Sit or lie down somewhere safe and comfortable. Keep the breath within a manageable range and return to natural breathing whenever needed.

Before you begin

Safety and contraindications

This session is for education and general wellbeing and is not medical advice. Do not practise while driving, in or near water, or anywhere a loss of consciousness could be dangerous. Do not practise if you are pregnant or have epilepsy, a cardiovascular condition or a serious respiratory condition. Consult a qualified healthcare professional if you are unsure whether breathwork is suitable for you.

Read the full safety guide →

If you feel uncomfortable, dizzy or unwell, stop the practice, return to natural breathing and rest.

The intention

A longer practice for sustained inward attention

The session extends the shorter psychedelic-inspired format into four rounds, with more time for quiet observation between active phases.

The active breathing is quick and continuous, while the holds create a strong contrast of stillness. The “psychedelic” description refers to the style and subjective quality of the practice, not a promised chemical or medical effect.

The four-round progression and its 3-6-9-inspired timing were used as a creative structural idea. Stay within your own limits and return to natural breathing whenever needed.

Welcome and technique

Hi, and welcome to another session with SpaceBreath. Today’s breathwork is an extended version of our psychedelic-inspired breathing. We will move through four rounds at a steady, fast pace.

At the end of each round, we will take a short hold on the inhale, followed by a longer hold on the exhale. The exhale holds build from 90 seconds in round one to two minutes 45 seconds in round four.

For the session, I recommend breathing in and out through the mouth. If that does not feel comfortable, adjust it to your preference. Please lie down and make sure you are in a safe environment.

Rounds one and two

Bring your full awareness into these slow, deep breaths. Fill your tummy first, draw the air into your chest and fully let go. One more, then we begin the faster breathing.

Stay present with your breath. Now take a deep inhale and hold. Engage gently from the pelvic floor through the abdomen and chest, then exhale. We stay here for 90 seconds.

Let’s move into round two. Keep the inhales strong and the exhales short. Take a full inhale and hold, then exhale. We stay here for one minute 45 seconds. Let go into relaxation and observe the sensations rising and passing.

Rounds three and four

Begin round three with the belly, chest and release. Connect with the sensations while remembering that you can reduce the pace or stop whenever needed.

Deep inhale and hold. Engage gently, then fully exhale. Relax your muscles, listen to the sounds and remain present through the hold.

For the final round, surrender to the rhythm while keeping the breath controlled. Take the deepest comfortable inhale of the session and hold, then exhale. We stay here for two minutes 45 seconds. Soften your shoulders, neck and face.

Closing

Three, two, one. Inhale and hold. Gently engage one last time, then exhale.

Give yourself time here. Watch the breath move softly in and out through your nose and enjoy this meditative state. Thank you for being part of today’s session. Keep sharing your love and peace with the world.

Quick answers

Before you begin

Practical answers about the pattern, structure and how to approach the session.

What does psychedelic-inspired breathwork mean here?

It describes the fast connected breathing style and the inward, altered quality some people may experience. The session does not promise a particular chemical or medical effect.

How long are the breath holds?

The inhale holds range from 30 to 45 seconds. The exhale holds progress from one minute 30 seconds to two minutes 45 seconds.

Is this suitable for beginners?

This is one of the stronger and longer SpaceBreath sessions. Beginners may prefer a shorter or gentler practice and should never force a hold.
