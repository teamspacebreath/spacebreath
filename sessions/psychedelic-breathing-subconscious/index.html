Session overview

At a glance

A slow, deep warm-up leads into three rounds of fast connected breathing, each with phases of fast breathing separated by a big inhale and a full exhale. Every round ends with a hold in two parts: an engaged inhale hold, then a relaxed hold on the exhale that grows from 1 minute 30 to 2 minutes 30. The session closes with a three-minute meditation.

Choosing your practice

Who this session is for

An intense session for people who already know how they respond to fast breathing and longer breath holds. If that's new to you, start with a gentler practice first.

Breathe with us

Before you begin

Safety and contraindications

This session is for education and general wellbeing and is not medical advice. Fast breathing and breath holds can cause tingling, light-headedness, dizziness, emotional release and, in rare cases, loss of consciousness. Always practise lying down or seated, and never while driving, operating machinery, or in or near water. Do not practise if you are pregnant or have epilepsy. If you have a cardiovascular or other serious medical condition, speak to a qualified healthcare professional first.

Read the full safety guide →

Never force a breath hold. When you need to breathe, take a recovery breath and return to natural breathing.

The breathing pattern

Three rounds.
Energy, then stillness.

The intention

Energy first, then stillness

The fast breathing builds energy. The long, relaxed exhale holds are where you let it settle and simply observe.

On each inhale hold, Jonas invites you to engage the pelvic floor, abdominals and chest and draw the energy upwards. On the exhale hold, everything softens: the head, shoulders, face and jaw. “Just be here in stillness. Listen, observe, feel.”

“Psychedelic” and “DMT breathing” describe the style and feel of the practice. Breathwork has not been shown to release DMT, and the session isn't a substitute for any substance or treatment.

The spoken introduction, hold guidance and closing. Repeated breathing cues are described rather than duplicated line by line.

Open transcriptEnglish · 23 minutes

Introduction

Hi, and welcome to another session with SpaceBreath. Today’s journey is a slightly different version of psychedelic breathing: three rounds in total. Each round moves through three phases of fast breathing, separated by a big inhale and a full exhale. It’s quite easy. Just follow the pattern. There’s nothing you can do wrong.

The breath hold at the end of each round comes in two parts. First on the inhale, with your body engaged, then a relaxation part with a hold on the exhale. Please lie down for this session. I recommend breathing in and out through your mouth, but if you prefer through your nose, that’s perfectly fine too.

Please also keep in mind that this is an intense session with fast breathing. If you don’t feel well at any point, don’t push it, and just return to your natural breathing in and out through your nose.

Warm-up and rounds

We begin with some slow, deep inhales as a warm-up. Slow, conscious inhales start in your tummy and are pulled up into your chest. Then the psychedelic breathing begins: tummy, chest, out. Focus on strong inhales, with no need to fully exhale. Move your body with every inhale. Make them explosive. Make them deep.

On each inhale hold: “Try to engage your pelvic floor, your abdominals, your chest. Bring this energy up.” On each exhale hold: “Bring full relaxation into your body. Try to relax your head, your shoulders, your face, your jaw. Just let go for a moment.”

Final hold and closing

“Just be here in stillness. Listen, observe, feel. Just enjoy this moment of peace.”

Let’s have a couple more inhales through the nose, and out through your mouth. Continue with your natural breathing, in and out through your nose, and stay in this meditative state for as long as you like. I hope you enjoyed the session. See you soon.

Quick answers

Before you begin

Practical answers about the pattern, the holds and how to approach the session.

How long are the breath holds?

Inhale holds of 33, 42 and 45 seconds, and exhale holds of 1 minute 30, 2 minutes and 2 minutes 30. Hold only for as long as feels comfortable.

Does this session release DMT?

No. “DMT breathing” is a popular name for this style of practice, but breathwork has not been shown to release DMT. Any effects come from the breathing itself.

Mouth or nose?

Jonas recommends breathing through the mouth for the fast phases, but the nose is perfectly fine if you prefer it.
