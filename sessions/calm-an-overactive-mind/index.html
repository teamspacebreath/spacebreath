Session overview

At a glance

This complete 16-minute video contains a 12-minute active practice within three guided rounds. Each round begins with slow belly-to-chest breathing, shifts to a faster pace and finishes with a 20-second inhale hold followed by an exhale hold.

Choosing your practice

Who this session is for

A focused practice for people familiar with active breathwork who want to step back from repetitive thoughts and reconnect with physical sensation.

Time

Sixteen-minute video

Intention

Calm and perspective

Created for moments when thoughts, doubts or emotions feel difficult to step away from.

Experience

Moderate intensity

Guided session

Breathe with us

Sit or lie down somewhere safe and comfortable. Keep the breath within a manageable range and return to natural breathing whenever needed.

Before you begin

Safety and contraindications

This session is for education and general wellbeing and is not medical advice. Do not practise while driving, in or near water, or anywhere a loss of consciousness could be dangerous. Do not practise if you are pregnant or have epilepsy, a cardiovascular condition or a serious respiratory condition. Consult a qualified healthcare professional if you are unsure whether breathwork is suitable for you.

Read the full safety guide →

If you feel uncomfortable, dizzy or unwell, stop the practice, return to natural breathing and rest.

The intention

A change of state through focused breathing

We cannot always change a situation immediately, but we can pause and change how we meet it.

The two breathing paces give attention a clear physical task. The slower phase establishes awareness, while the faster phase creates energy before the quieter exhale hold.

Approach the session without expecting a particular result. Notice what is present, keep the breathing controllable and let the practice be an opportunity to observe rather than react.

Open transcriptEnglish · 16 minutes

Welcome and technique

Hi, and welcome to another session with SpaceBreath. Today we will move through three rounds of breathing that can change how you feel in about 12 minutes. Try not to hold expectations. Keep an open mind and stay curious.

We breathe in through the nose and out through the mouth, first into the tummy and then into the chest. Each round uses two different paces. At the end, we take a short hold after the inhale and a longer hold after the exhale.

Please sit upright or lie down. We begin with slow, conscious inhales in three, two, one.

Round one

Feel the movement in your body and let it move with your breath. Two more before we go faster. Stay in the rhythm: tummy, chest, out.

Take a strong, deep inhale and hold for a moment. Fully release. We stay here for one minute 15 seconds. Listen to the sounds, notice the sensations and observe.

Rounds two and three

Let’s move into round two with slow, deep inhales. Bring full awareness to each breath. Feel the air entering through your nostrils and the stretch through your tummy and chest.

Take a full inhale and hold. Exhale, then stay here for one minute 30 seconds. Let go of tension and check where the shoulders, neck, head or jaw can relax further.

For the final round, stay present and move consciously with every breath. After the faster phase, take your deepest comfortable inhale and hold. Fully release and rest through the final exhale hold.

Closing

Deep inhale and hold, then release. Slowly return to the space around you. Continue watching the breath as it moves in and out through the nose.

Some things in life cannot be changed immediately, but we can choose how we perceive them and what we bring into the world. I hope this practice brought you some peace. See you soon.

Quick answers

Before you begin

Practical answers about the pattern, structure and how to approach the session.

Why does the title say 12 minutes when the video is longer?

The central active breathwork lasts approximately 12 minutes. The complete video, including the introduction and closing integration, is just over 16 minutes.

How does each round work?

Each round starts with slow belly-to-chest breathing, moves to a faster pace, then closes with a short inhale hold and a longer exhale hold.

Can I stop before the timer ends?

Yes. The hold times are guidance, not targets. Return to natural breathing as soon as your body asks you to.
