Session overview

At a glance

This 24-minute session contains three rounds of conscious breathing. Each round starts at a slower pace, shifts to a faster rhythm and ends with a 20-second inhale hold followed by exhale holds of one minute 15 seconds, one minute 45 seconds and two minutes 15 seconds.

Choosing your practice

Who this session is for

A longer emotional-reset practice for people comfortable with active breathing who want to explore gratitude, ease and a quieter mental state.

Guided session

Breathe with us

Sit or lie down somewhere safe and comfortable. Keep the breath within a manageable range and return to natural breathing whenever needed.

Before you begin

Safety and contraindications

This session is for education and general wellbeing and is not medical advice. Do not practise while driving, in or near water, or anywhere a loss of consciousness could be dangerous. Do not practise if you are pregnant or have epilepsy, a cardiovascular condition or a serious respiratory condition. Consult a qualified healthcare professional if you are unsure whether breathwork is suitable for you.

If you feel uncomfortable, dizzy or unwell, stop the practice, return to natural breathing and rest.

The intention

Make room for simple positive moments

The session uses breath, attention and stillness as an invitation to notice ease, gratitude and the possibility of joy.

The guidance encourages a gentle smile, awareness of the body and appreciation for the simple experience of breathing. These are reflective prompts, not a promise to change mood chemistry or treat a health condition.

Each round creates contrast between active breathing and quiet holding. Allow whatever is present without forcing happiness or measuring the experience against an expected result.

About the music

Three musical chapters moving towards lightness.

The original score is built at 96 BPM and changes character across the three rounds. A pulsing, monochromatic opening gives way to a more optimistic four-part chord pattern, before all of the musical elements converge in the final round.

Welcome and intention

Hi, and welcome to another session with SpaceBreath. Today’s journey is about reconnecting with the little moments when the mind becomes quiet and a gentle smile appears.

We move through three rounds, using two breathing paces in each. Every round ends with a short hold on the inhale followed by a longer hold on the exhale, building from one minute 15 seconds to two minutes 15 seconds.

Please lie down and make yourself comfortable. We begin by breathing gently into the tummy, then drawing the breath into the chest.

Round one

Follow the rhythm. Notice the movement in the tummy and chest and allow the shoulders to relax. Inhale presence and exhale tension.

Take a deep inhale and hold at the top. Softly engage the pelvic floor, then fully release. We hold here for one minute 15 seconds. Feel into the body and bring a gentle smile to your face.

Rounds two and three

For round two, feel gratitude for simply being here and experiencing your breath. Allow the body to soften and welcome a sense of safety and peace.

After the faster breathing, inhale and hold. Release into relaxation and stay after the exhale for one minute 45 seconds. Relax the face, shoulders and tummy.

Move into the final round with this positivity. Stay close to yourself and notice every detail of the breath. After the active phase, inhale, hold and let go. We stay here for two minutes 15 seconds.

Closing

Smile, listen to the music and notice where you can relax further. Begin with the face and jaw, then move through the neck, shoulders and the rest of the body.

Deep inhale, hold for a moment and fully exhale. Return to natural breathing through the nose. Thank you for being part of today’s session. Stay positive and share this feeling with the world. See you soon.

Quick answers

Before you begin

Practical answers about the pattern, structure and how to approach the session.

What is the intention of this session?

The guidance uses breath, gratitude and quiet observation as invitations to reconnect with ease and positive feeling. It does not promise a medical or biochemical result.

How long are the exhale holds?

The three exhale holds last approximately one minute 15 seconds, one minute 45 seconds and two minutes 15 seconds.

Do I have to feel happy during the practice?

No. There is no required emotional outcome. Notice what is present and let the breath remain a supportive point of attention.
