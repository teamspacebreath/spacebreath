Session overview

At a glance

This 25-minute session contains four rounds of active tummy-to-chest breathing. Each round finishes with a hold after the exhale, progressing through one minute, one minute 30 seconds, two minutes and two minutes 30 seconds.

Choosing your practice

Who this session is for

A longer practice for people already familiar with active breathwork who want to explore increasingly spacious holds without treating the timer as a target.

Time

Intention

Deep reset

Experience

Moderate to strong

Guided session

Breathe with us

Sit or lie down somewhere safe and comfortable. Keep the breath within a manageable range and return to natural breathing whenever needed.

Before you begin

Safety and contraindications

This session is for education and general wellbeing and is not medical advice. Do not practise while driving, in or near water, or anywhere a loss of consciousness could be dangerous. Do not practise if you are pregnant or have epilepsy, a cardiovascular condition or a serious respiratory condition. Consult a qualified healthcare professional if you are unsure whether breathwork is suitable for you.

Read the full safety guide →

If you feel uncomfortable, dizzy or unwell, stop the practice, return to natural breathing and rest.

The intention

A longer practice built around stillness

The extended holds create more quiet between rounds, but they remain optional time guides rather than goals to achieve.

This is an independent SpaceBreath practice inspired by Wim Hof-style breathing, not an official Wim Hof Method exercise. The focus is on a deep inhale followed by a soft release.

Tingling, heaviness, light-headedness or other sensations can occur. They should not be described as universally harmless. Stop, return to natural breathing and rest if you feel unwell.

Welcome and technique

Hi, and welcome to another session with SpaceBreath. Today we are doing an extended version of standard Wim Hof-style breathing with four rounds and longer breath holds.

Focus on making each inhale deep. Begin in the tummy and expand into the chest. The exhale is a soft release rather than an active emptying of the lungs. You may breathe through the mouth or nose, depending on what feels more comfortable.

The holds can be long and should never be forced. If you need to breathe sooner, take a deep inhale, hold briefly and continue with gentle natural breathing. Lie down if possible, or sit upright with a straight back.

Rounds one and two

Begin with steady tummy-to-chest breaths. Keep the exhale soft. After the final breath, inhale fully, exhale completely and rest after the exhale for one minute. Relax the shoulders and face.

Take a recovery inhale, hold briefly and release. Round two follows the same deep breathing pattern. After the final exhale, stay for up to one minute 30 seconds. Notice the body without trying to change the sensations.

Rounds three and four

Return to deep breathing for round three. Stay close to the breath and keep every inhale full. Exhale completely and rest for up to two minutes. Breathe sooner if needed.

For the final round, use the same tummy-chest-release rhythm. After the last inhale, exhale fully and rest for up to two minutes 30 seconds. Let the body soften instead of working against the need to breathe.

Closing

Take a full recovery inhale, hold briefly and exhale. Return to natural breathing through the nose.

Thank you for showing up and sharing the practice. Take your time before standing and carry the grounded feeling into the rest of your day. See you soon.

Quick answers

Before you begin

Practical answers about the pattern, structure and how to approach the session.

Is this an official Wim Hof Method session?

No. It is an independent SpaceBreath practice inspired by a similar breathing style.

How long are the breath holds?

The holds after the exhale progress from one minute to one minute 30 seconds, two minutes and two minutes 30 seconds.

Do I need to complete every hold?

No. End any hold as soon as your body needs air. The timer is guidance, not a target.
