Session overview

At a glance

This complete nine-minute video contains approximately seven minutes of active breathwork. It includes three quick rounds, each moving from a regular pace through a faster middle phase to a fast final phase. The rounds end with exhale holds of 30, 30 and 60 seconds.

Before you begin

Safety and contraindications

This session is for education and general wellbeing and is not medical advice. Do not practise while driving, in or near water, or anywhere a loss of consciousness could be dangerous. Do not practise if you are pregnant or have epilepsy, a cardiovascular condition or a serious respiratory condition. Consult a qualified healthcare professional if you are unsure whether breathwork is suitable for you.

If you feel uncomfortable, dizzy or unwell, stop the practice, return to natural breathing and rest.

The intention

A short progression from rhythm to stillness

Three changing paces keep the active phases concise, while the exhale holds provide a contrasting pause after each round.

The session takes inspiration from Wim Hof-style breathing but is a SpaceBreath practice rather than an official Wim Hof Method exercise. Mouth breathing is suggested, but the pace should always stay within your control.

The energising language describes the intended experience, not a guaranteed physiological outcome. Stop if you feel unwell or the intensity becomes difficult to manage.

About the music

Chords generated from a photograph of a lake.

The musical starting point came from analysing the pixels in a lake photograph and mapping groups of pixels to frequency ranges. Those values were converted into chords, then developed in Logic Pro into an original score for the three changing breathing paces.

Welcome and technique

Hi, and welcome to another session with SpaceBreath. Today’s session is a short but energising practice, suitable for daily life whenever you need a boost.

We will move through three rounds. In every round, we breathe at three different paces, beginning at a regular rhythm, building into a faster breath and finishing with a hold after the exhale.

I recommend practising upright and breathing in and out through the mouth. Focus on making each inhale deep, followed by only a short exhale, like a quick release.

Round one

Follow the breathing pattern with full focus on deep inhales. We go a little faster, then accelerate again. Stay with the rhythm.

Take a deep inhale, fully release and stay after the exhale. Three, two, one. Deep inhale and hold for a moment. Squeeze the upper body, then fully release.

Rounds two and three

Begin round two with the same pattern and keep the focus on deep inhales. Accelerate once, then move faster again. Take a deep inhale, fully let go and stay after the exhale. Observe and release any tension in the body.

For the final round, stay with the rhythm and go deep with the breath. Accelerate through the three paces. Take a deep inhale, release completely and stay after the exhale. Fully let go into the hold and feel your body.

Closing

Three, two, one. Deep inhale and hold for a moment. Give the body one last comfortable squeeze and release.

Slowly return to the space around you. I hope you feel energised and fresh after this short session. Enjoy the rest of your day and see you soon.
