Session overview

At a glance

This 25-minute session uses four rounds. The first two build through changing breathing speeds and finish with short inhale holds. The last two slow the pace and finish with exhale holds of two minutes and two minutes 30 seconds.

Choosing your practice

Who this session is for

A longer, energising practice for people familiar with active breathwork who want intensity followed by a deliberate cool-down.

Guided session

Breathe with us

Sit or lie down somewhere safe and comfortable. Keep the breath within a manageable range and return to natural breathing whenever needed.

Before you begin

Safety and contraindications

This session is for education and general wellbeing and is not medical advice. Do not practise while driving, in or near water, or anywhere a loss of consciousness could be dangerous. Do not practise if you are pregnant or have epilepsy, a cardiovascular condition or a serious respiratory condition. Consult a qualified healthcare professional if you are unsure whether breathwork is suitable for you.

If you feel uncomfortable, dizzy or unwell, stop the practice, return to natural breathing and rest.

The intention

Intensity with an intentional cool-down

The practice gives the first half to active breathing and the second half to slower breath and longer stillness.

The session is inspired by Wim Hof-style power breathing but is an independent SpaceBreath practice, not an official Wim Hof Method exercise.

Treat the muscular engagement during inhale holds as light and controlled. During the longer exhale holds, breathe sooner whenever your body asks for air rather than competing with the timer.

Welcome and technique

Hi, and welcome to another session with SpaceBreath. Today we are moving through four rounds of power breathing.

The first two rounds are more intense. We breathe actively for longer, move through different speeds and finish with a short hold after the inhale. The final two rounds are a cool-down with slower, deeper breathing and exhale holds of up to two minutes 30 seconds.

Lie down or sit upright. Breathe deeply into the belly and chest, followed by a short, soft release.

Rounds one and two

Begin slowly and deeply, filling the belly and chest. Increase the breathing speed in two stages while keeping the inhale full.

At the end, take a complete inhale and hold. Gently engage the legs, pelvic floor, abdomen and upper body, then release.

Round two repeats the progression. Stay present as the pace rises, then finish with another short inhale hold and comfortable full-body engagement.

Rounds three and four

For round three, slow everything down. Take deep, conscious breaths, release fully and rest after the exhale for two minutes. Let the face, shoulders and neck soften. Breathe sooner if needed.

The final round begins slowly and rises once more. After the last deep inhale, exhale completely and rest for up to two minutes 30 seconds. Keep the body relaxed and let the timing remain secondary to comfort.

Closing

Take a deep inhale, hold briefly and fully release. Return to natural breathing through the nose.

Move the fingers and toes and gradually reconnect with the space around you. Thank you for joining the practice. See you soon.

Quick answers

Before you begin

Practical answers about the pattern, structure and how to approach the session.

Is this an official Wim Hof Method session?

No. It is an independent SpaceBreath practice inspired by a similar power-breathing style.

How do the four rounds differ?

The first two are longer and more intense and finish after the inhale. The final two slow down and finish with long exhale holds.

Do I need to complete every hold?

No. The times are guides. Breathe sooner and return to natural breathing whenever needed.
