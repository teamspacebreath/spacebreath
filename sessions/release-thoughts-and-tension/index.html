Before you begin

Practise safely

This session is for educational purposes only. Do not practise if you are pregnant or have a history of epilepsy or cardiovascular issues. Never practise near water, while driving or while operating machinery. By taking part, you accept responsibility for your own wellbeing.

Tingling, dizziness or emotional release can sometimes occur during breathwork. Pause the session and return to natural breathing if you feel unwell.

The intention

Making room to let go

We created this session to bring calm to the body and make room for release, one layer at a time.

The idea grew from a personal reflection: even when the mind moves on from difficult moments, feelings can still seem present in the body. This kind of breathing helps us pause, reconnect and meet what we are carrying with more softness.

There is nothing to force. Observe what arises, feel what is here and allow the longer exhale to create a little more space.

Welcome and intention

Hi, and welcome to another session with SpaceBreath. Today’s journey is all about letting go. It might be thoughts. It might be tension in the body built up from different situations in our lives.

The mind might forget, but the body always remembers. Today is the day to release these things by observing, feeling and eventually letting go of them.

We will do three rounds in total, starting with a slower pace in through the nose and out through the mouth, then consciously accelerating at some point and finishing each round with a relaxed breath hold on the exhale.

All right. Please sit upright or lie down for this session, and we will begin in three, two, one.

Round one

Out. Soft and conscious breathing out.

Feel the air entering through your nostrils and leaving over your lips.

Relax a little more with every exhale. Start with your face. Relax your jaw, your eyes, your cheeks. It’s okay if you make a little sound with every exhale.

Now your shoulders and neck, let them drop. Try to release a little more with every breath. Bring your awareness to your chest, your tummy. Let them open. Let them sink. Feel your hands. Feel your fingers. Feel your legs. Feel your feet. Just be aware. Let go. Just be here.

One more breath, and then we will slightly pick up the pace. Stay in the rhythm. Keep this relaxation inside you.

Out. Out. Out.

Okay, the last two from here.

Out. Out.

And now take a slow and deep inhale through your nose. And fully let go through your mouth. We stay here for 45 seconds.

Feel the change that is happening in your body. Just observe. Just be.

Three, two, one. Inhale. Stay here for a moment. And release.

Round two

All right, let’s begin with our second round, starting in three, two, one.

Remember to stay in full relaxation mode. Focus on your face. Relax your jaw, your cheeks, your eyes. Relax your shoulders, your neck. Just let go of any tension you might hold.

Out. Out.

Okay, the last one before we go faster.

Out. Out.

Time your breath. Make it a rhythm. Make it one movement.

Out. Out. Out.

Remember to stay conscious. Be here. Be with your breath.

In, out. In, out. In, out.

All right. Now, deep inhale through your nose. Nice and slow. And let go. We stay here for one minute.

Three, two, one. Inhale. Hold here for a moment. And release.

Round three

Let’s start with our final round in three, two, one.

Bring your full presence into this last round. Feel your body. Notice your relaxed face, your jaw, your hands, your feet. Notice how lightness spreads through you.

Out. Out. Out.

Okay, our last breath before we go slightly faster.

Out. In, out. Out. Out.

Make it one continuous flow. Strong inhales, slow exhales.

Out. Out. Out. Out. Out. Out.

And now one last long and deep inhale through your nose. And exhale. We hold here for one minute and 15 seconds.

Whatever comes up, always come back to your feeling, the feeling of your body, of the space around you, and just be here.

Three, two, one. Inhale. Hold here for a moment. And fully let go.

Closing

Continue watching your breath in and out through your nose. Stay here as long as you like in this meditative state.

Thank you for joining us today. Enjoy the rest of your day, and see you soon.
