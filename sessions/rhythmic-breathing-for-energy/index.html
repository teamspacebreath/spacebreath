Session overview

At a glance

This eight-minute practice contains three rounds of rhythmic mouth breathing. The repeating pattern is inhale, exhale, double inhale, long exhale. The rounds finish with exhale holds of 30 seconds, 45 seconds and one minute.

Before you begin

Safety and contraindications

This session is for education and general wellbeing and is not medical advice. Do not practise while driving, in or near water, or anywhere a loss of consciousness could be dangerous. Do not practise if you are pregnant or have epilepsy, a cardiovascular condition or a serious respiratory condition. Consult a qualified healthcare professional if you are unsure whether breathwork is suitable for you.

If you feel uncomfortable, dizzy or unwell, stop the practice, return to natural breathing and rest.

The intention

Let repetition create momentum

The unusual rhythm gives the mind a clear sequence while the body gradually builds warmth and energy.

Each cycle begins with a single inhale and exhale, then a double inhale that moves from the belly into the chest before one longer exhale.

The holds become longer across the three rounds, but they are not targets to compete with. Return to natural breathing whenever you need to.

Welcome and technique

Hi, and welcome to another session with SpaceBreath. Today we are practising rhythmic breathing with a pattern of inhale, exhale, then a double inhale followed by one long exhale, all through your mouth.

This rhythm is intended to build energy and warmth in your body and create a sense of expansion from within. Make yourself comfortable, and we begin in three, two, one.

Round one

Feel into every breath. Let your tummy and chest move with each inhale and exhale.

The last one. Take a deep inhale and fully let go. We hold here for 30 seconds.

Keep holding, and we will go straight into our second round in three, two, one.

Rounds two and three

Go as deep as feels manageable with your inhales, and let go with your exhales.

Now take a strong inhale and fully let go. We hold here for 45 seconds.

Hold the breath, and we will continue with our last round in three, two, one. Move your body with each breath. Feel the energy you are inhaling.

Take a strong inhale. Fully release. We hold here for one minute.

Closing

Three, two, one. Inhale and hold here for a moment. Fully let go.

Return to your natural breathing through your nose. Notice each breath coming in and out. Thank you for being here today. Enjoy your space and enjoy your breath. See you soon.

Quick answers

Before you begin

Practical answers about the pattern, structure and how to approach the session.

What is the rhythmic breathing pattern?

The repeating pattern is inhale, exhale, double inhale, then one longer exhale, all through the mouth.

How many rounds are included?

There are three rounds, followed by exhale holds of 30 seconds, 45 seconds and one minute.

Do I need to match the rhythm perfectly?

No. Use the music and guidance as reference points, but reduce the pace or return to natural breathing whenever needed.
