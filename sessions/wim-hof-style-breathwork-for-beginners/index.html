Session overview

At a glance

This 18-minute session contains three rounds of deep tummy-to-chest breathing. The rounds finish with exhale holds of 45 seconds, one minute 15 seconds and one minute 30 seconds, each followed by a brief inhale hold.

Before you begin

Safety and contraindications

This session is for education and general wellbeing and is not medical advice. Do not practise while driving, in or near water, or anywhere a loss of consciousness could be dangerous. Do not practise if you are pregnant or have epilepsy, a cardiovascular condition or a serious respiratory condition. Consult a qualified healthcare professional if you are unsure whether breathwork is suitable for you.

If you feel uncomfortable, dizzy or unwell, stop the practice, return to natural breathing and rest.

The breathing pattern

Three rounds.
Tummy, chest, release.

Fill the tummy first and then the chest. Let the exhale release softly rather than forcing it. Every round finishes with a rest after the exhale.

The intention

A clear introduction to active breathing

The tummy-chest-release pattern stays consistent across all three rounds while the quiet holds increase gradually.

This is an independent SpaceBreath practice inspired by Wim Hof-style breathing, not an official Wim Hof Method exercise. Nose or mouth breathing can be used according to comfort.

Beginner-friendly does not mean sensation-free. Tingling or light-headedness can occur. Stop immediately and return to natural breathing if you feel unwell.

Welcome and technique

Hi, and welcome to SpaceBreath. Today we are doing three rounds of Wim Hof-style breathing designed as an accessible introduction.

Focus on the inhale. Breathe first into the tummy, then expand into the chest. The exhale is quick and soft, more like a release than a full active exhale.

At the end of each round, we hold after the exhale, followed by a brief hold after the recovery inhale. Breathe through the nose or mouth according to comfort. Lie down safely if possible, or sit upright.

Round one

Begin with deep tummy-to-chest inhales and a soft release. Stay with the steady rhythm.

After the final breath, inhale fully, exhale completely and rest after the exhale for up to 45 seconds. Let go of tension in the shoulders, neck, face and jaw.

Take a full recovery inhale, hold briefly with gentle abdominal engagement, then exhale.

Rounds two and three

Round two repeats the same deep breathing pattern. After the final exhale, rest for up to one minute 15 seconds. Notice the body and allow it to relax.

In the final round, keep each inhale deep and the rhythm controlled. Exhale fully and rest for up to one minute 30 seconds. End the hold earlier whenever your body needs air.

Closing

Take a full inhale, hold briefly and release. Return to natural nasal breathing.

Thank you for being present. I hope you feel calm and refreshed. Have a wonderful day, keep smiling and spread kindness wherever you go. See you soon.

Quick answers

Before you begin

Practical answers about the pattern, structure and how to approach the session.

Is this suitable for complete beginners?

It is designed as an accessible introduction, but every pace and hold remains optional. Stop or shorten the practice whenever needed.

How long are the holds?

The exhale holds last up to 45 seconds, one minute 15 seconds and one minute 30 seconds. Each is followed by a brief recovery inhale hold.

Is this an official Wim Hof Method session?

No. It is an independent SpaceBreath practice inspired by a similar active breathing style.
